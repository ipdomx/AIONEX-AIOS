# AFS-0000 — Foundation

## Mission

Build a trusted Engineering Intelligence Operating System that amplifies engineers rather than replacing them.

## Core principles

1. Truth before confidence.
2. Evidence before decisions.
3. Human owns the final decision.
4. Explain important decisions.
5. Never repeat verified failures.
6. Security by design.
7. No silent high-impact actions.
8. Workspaces before live modifications.
9. Everything important must be testable and auditable.
10. AFS is the source of architectural truth.
11. Self-updates require isolation, tests, rollback, and policy approval.
12. Vendor lock-in must be avoided through stable provider interfaces.
