# ADR-0004 — Adopt Federated Cognitive Cells

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
The 1.0 expert council returns static opinions and does not provide independent reasoning, evidence, persistent deliberation, or voting.

## Decision
Replace the static council over time with isolated, policy-governed cognitive cells coordinated through a deliberation protocol.

## Consequences
- Decisions become explainable, reviewable, and auditable.
- Cells can be upgraded or replaced independently.
- More orchestration and conflict-resolution logic is required.
- Institutional deployments can enable different cells and policies.
