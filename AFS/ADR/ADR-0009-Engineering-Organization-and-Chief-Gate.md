# ADR-0009: Engineering Organization and Chief Project Engineer Gate

## Status
Accepted for AIOS v1.2.0-alpha.6.

## Decision
AIOS models project delivery as a digital engineering organization. Every participating department must provide a specialist, senior engineer, and manager. Department managers approve only evidence-backed deliverables. A Chief Project Engineer performs the final cross-department review and withholds release when any acceptance, testing, security, rollback, or evidence requirement remains incomplete.

## Consequences
- Complex work is decomposed by accountable specialization.
- Delivery cannot be declared complete by the producing worker alone.
- Incomplete work returns with an explicit rework plan.
- Competency and authority are represented independently, enabling future reputation, recruitment, and evolution engines.
