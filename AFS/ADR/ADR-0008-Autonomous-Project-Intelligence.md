# ADR-0008: Autonomous Project Intelligence

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
AIOS will model every project before mutation through a deterministic Digital Twin and persistent Knowledge Graph. Significant actions are evaluated by a provider-independent Constitution Engine. Competing strategies are ranked by a Wisdom Engine emphasizing evidence, security, reversibility, maintainability, and long-term value. Defensive analysis requires explicit authorization and produces remediation and test plans rather than exploit workflows.

## Consequences
- AI model vendors remain replaceable plugins.
- Project understanding and institutional knowledge survive model changes.
- Weakly evidenced decisions may be rejected or deferred.
- Multi-domain tasks can run concurrently while failures remain isolated.
- Future connectors can enrich the graph without redesigning the core.
