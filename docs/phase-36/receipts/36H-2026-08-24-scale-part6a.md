# Phase 36H.6A — Isolated 1000-client backplane/failover acceptance

Source-only acceptance for the distributed websocket/backplane layer. The test creates 1,000 synthetic clients across four hubs and ten tenants, verifies tenant-isolated fanout, exercises loss of one node, reconnects affected clients to a surviving node, and verifies recovery delivery with no stale failed-node subscription.

This receipt does **not** claim live SFU/TURN/media scale, public-port readiness, production migrations, recording failover, or production readiness. LiveKit, Coturn, Egress, provider requests, GPU jobs and production mutation remain disabled/out of scope.
