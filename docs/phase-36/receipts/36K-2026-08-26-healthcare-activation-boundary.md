# Phase 36K — Healthcare Administration Activation Boundary

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: **PASS — internal source boundary retained; jurisdictional activation gate made explicit**

The Phase 36K healthcare administration blueprint is intentionally non-diagnostic and excludes autonomous diagnosis, prescription, treatment selection and clinical disposition. The internal source path covers registration/consent, appointment scheduling, records access/audit, professional evidence review, and retention/deletion review.

The 2026-08-25 high-stakes receipt explicitly states that healthcare administration remains `source_built` and that no jurisdictional compliance certification is claimed. The final registry now records that missing prerequisite directly as:

`jurisdictional-healthcare-compliance-certification`

This is an external activation gate because the applicable legal, regulatory, data-residency, clinical-governance and deployment requirements depend on the jurisdiction and operator. It must not be fabricated by the software project.

No clinical autonomy, provider request, external clinical API, or Production clinical-data mutation is claimed by this reconciliation.
