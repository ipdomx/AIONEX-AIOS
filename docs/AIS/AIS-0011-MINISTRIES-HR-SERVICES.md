# AIS-0011 — Ministries, Career, Academy, and Service Boundaries

Phase 2 adds isolated ministries, a digital-workforce career system, an academy, and a universal service registry.

Rules:
1. Ministries do not call each other's internals.
2. Owner approval is required for service activation and career promotion.
3. New services are researched and evaluated before activation.
4. Discovery never means automatic installation.
5. Service policies can be scoped to system, organization, project, team, or worker.
